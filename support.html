Dash Support

Dash helps parents keep an eye on their kid while they ride an e-bike, scooter, or anything else with a motor — live location during rides, speed alerts, safety zones, and sign/traffic-light awareness.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up my family?
Create an account, choose the Parent role, then add a child profile from the Parent dashboard. Each child gets their own profile with a name and contact phone number.
How does ride tracking work?
When your child starts a ride in their app, Dash tracks their live location, speed, and route for the duration of the ride so you can follow along and get alerts if something looks off.
Why does Dash need location access?
Location is used only while a ride is active, to show the route, check speed against the limit you set, and power safety-zone and sign-awareness alerts. See the Privacy Policy for details.
What is "Screenshot demo mode"?
A built-in scripted demo ride (found on the Parent Account & PIN screen) that lets you preview the live tracking, speed alerts, and sign-awareness features without needing to actually ride anywhere.
How do I delete my account or data?
In the app, open Parent Account & PIN, scroll to the Delete account card, and confirm with your account password. This permanently deletes your family's account, kids' profiles, ride history, alerts, and pairing data from our servers and your device. You can also email us at the address below and we'll delete it for you.
A stop sign, traffic light, yield sign, or crosswalk is missing from my map. Can I report it?
Yes. Dash finds signs from OpenStreetMap, a free public map, and not every sign has been added there yet. In the app, open the menu, choose Help & Support, then Report a missing sign. Pick the type, stand next to the sign, tap "Use my current location," and send. We review reports and add the real signs to OpenStreetMap so every rider benefits. Only the sign type, its location, and your optional note are sent, with nothing about you or your family. You can also add signs to OpenStreetMap yourself at openstreetmap.org.
Where do I find help inside the app?
Open the menu and choose Help & Support. You'll find the missing-sign report form, this support page, and the Privacy Policy there.
