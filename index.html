Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Dash ("the App") is a mobile app that helps parents keep track of a child's e-bike, scooter, or other motorized-vehicle rides. This policy explains what information Dash collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you create a Dash account, we collect the email address and password you provide. Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Location data: While a ride is in progress, Dash collects precise GPS location (including briefly in the background while the screen is locked) to track the route, measure speed, and provide safety-zone and sign/traffic-light alerts. Location is only collected during an active ride, not continuously in the background at all other times.
  • Family/account data: Information you enter to set up a family, such as a parent and child profile, ride history, and safety-zone boundaries you define.

How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to provide the App's core features: live ride tracking, speed alerts, safety-zone notifications, and ride history for the parent account. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How Information Is Shared

Ride and location data for a child's account is visible to the linked parent account within the same family, by design. Dash does not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), which processes data on our behalf under its own security and compliance practices.

Data Retention

We retain account and ride data for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app (Parent Account & PIN, then Delete account), or by contacting us (see below). Deleting your account permanently removes it from our servers and from your device.

Children's Privacy

Dash is designed to be used by a family unit: a parent creates and manages the family account, and a child's ride data is visible to that parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child independent of a parent-managed family account. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided information outside of this context, please contact us so we can address it.

Your Choices

  • Location access can be turned off at any time in your device's system settings, though this will prevent ride tracking from functioning.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app (Parent Account & PIN, then Delete account), or by contacting us at the email below.

Security

We use industry-standard practices, including Firebase's built-in authentication and database security rules, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
